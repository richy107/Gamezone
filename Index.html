🖥️ How to Identify a Gaming PC

🎮 Graphics Card

  • Min: GTX 1660 / RX 580
  • Rec: RTX 3060 Ti+
  • VRAM: 6GB min, 8GB+ rec

⚡ Processor

  • Min: Intel i5 / Ryzen 5
  • Rec: Intel i7 / Ryzen 7
  • 3.5GHz+ clock speed

💾 RAM

  • Min: 16GB DDR4
  • Rec: 32GB DDR4/DDR5
  • 3200MHz+ speed

💽 Storage

  • NVMe SSD essential
  • Min: 500GB SSD
  • Rec: 1TB+ NVMe

🔌 Power Supply

  • Min: 650W Bronze
  • Rec: 750W-850W Gold
  • Modular cables

🌬️ Cooling

  • Aftermarket CPU cooler
  • Good airflow case
  • 3+ case fans

🖥️ Monitor

  • 1080p 144Hz min
  • 1440p 144Hz+ rec
  • 1ms response time

⚠️ Red Flags

  • No dedicated GPU
  • 8GB RAM or less
  • HDD primary drive
